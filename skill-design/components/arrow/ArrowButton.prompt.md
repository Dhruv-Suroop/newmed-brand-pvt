**ArrowButton** — CTAs in the brand arrow shape, as used on the site and deck ("Partner with us", "Book a workforce discussion").

```jsx
<ArrowButton size="lg">Book a workforce discussion</ArrowButton>
<ArrowButton outline size="lg">See how it works</ArrowButton>
```

Primary = orange fill; `outline` for secondary. The pill `Button` remains for quiet product-UI actions; marketing/deck CTAs use ArrowButton.
