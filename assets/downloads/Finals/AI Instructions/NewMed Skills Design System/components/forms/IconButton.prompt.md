**IconButton** — icon-only square button for toolbars, nav, and close/dismiss actions. Always pass `aria-label`.

```jsx
<IconButton aria-label="Menu"><MenuIcon/></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="solid" aria-label="Next">→</IconButton>
```

Variants: `solid` (coral), `ghost`, `outline`. Rounded-square (`--radius-md`). Press scales to 0.94.
