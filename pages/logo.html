The mark.

An N constructed from arrows. Forward motion made from the letter itself. It carries the ambition and optimism of moving forward, and also creating momentum to make real progress. Give it room, keep it in its approved colours, and never redraw it.

Clear space

Clear space is the area around the logo that stays empty at all times, so the mark is always distinguishable from surrounding graphics. The x in these diagrams marks the minimum clear space for each lockup.

Minimum sizeSymbol: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Stack lockup: 140 px or 28 mm by width. Below this the arrow detail closes up and the wordmark stops reading.

Partnerships

When locking up with a partner, use the primary logo in orange and brown, or single colour cream, and a monochrome version of the partner mark wherever one exists. Where the partner mark is a symbol and the words are not needed, our symbol may stand in for the full lockup. Clear space rules carry over, and a rule line divides the two marks.

Incorrect usage

The mark is fixed. If a situation seems to need one of these, it needs a different lockup instead.

✗
Don't recolourOnly approved brand colours.
✗
Don't rotateKeep it upright, always.
✗
Don't stretchScale proportionally only.
✗
Don't add effectsNo shadows, glows or gradients.
✗
Don't place on clutterKeep the ground clean and high contrast.
✗
Don't outline or boxNo added strokes or containers.
