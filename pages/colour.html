A grounded, warm palette.

Creating moments of softness and humanity. It feels warm beyond just coolness, reassuring and calming because of the gradients. Orange is used as a signal, a moment, not a wash. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Orange

Orange is the hero of the palette and the one colour that carries the brand on its own. Use it where it means something: a link, a state, a single call to move. Used with restraint it holds the most weight, so it is never washed across a whole surface.

Secondary

Deep Brown carries every word of text and every dark surface: a warm near-black, never a pure black. Yellow extends the warmth beside orange, Maroon adds depth for serious moments, and Teal is the one cool note and the most sparing of all.

Using the colours together

Which colours is only half the battle. We use the darker and more saturated colours very minimally, keeping it mostly light, soft, subtle, and calming. This is how we balance proportions.

Default (70% Off white, 20% Orange, 10% Maroon)
Variant (70% Maroon, 10% Orange, 10% Yellow, 10% Off white)

Gradients

Gradients are always organic, soft and flowing: layered off-centre washes fading into cream, never hard-edged circles and never a colour from outside the palette. They carry ambient warmth behind a hero statement, and they never compete with the words on top of them.

The organic gradientThe master wash. Yellow and orange bleed off the edges and resolve into cream, with no visible centre and no hard edge anywhere.

How a radial is built

Each radial runs from a single palette colour at full strength out to cream, across a long falloff. The construction below marks the two endpoints. Keep the falloff generous: a short one produces a visible ring, which reads as a hard edge.

Colour modes

No single mode carries the full palette. Each medium has a preferred mode, so match the values to where the work will live.

ModeApplicationNotes
HEX / RGBScreenThe truest, most vibrant depiction of the palette. Use for every digital surface.
PantonePrintPreferred for print. Matches the brand colours most reliably across stocks. Official values to be added.
CMYKPrintUse only when Pantone is unavailable, matching to Pantone where possible. Official values to be added.

Accessibility

Deep Brown on Cream clears WCAG AA for body text. Orange is for large text, icons and accents. Pair small text with deep brown or cream, never orange on orange.

Incorrect usage

The palette is fixed. Keep colours pure, keep contrast high, and let Orange stay the accent.
