Warm, with one cool note.

A grounded, warm palette does the everyday work. Signal Orange is used as a signal, a moment, not a wash. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Signal Orange

Signal Orange is the hero. It appears where it means something: a link, a state, a single call to move. Used with restraint it carries the most weight, so it is never washed across a whole surface.

Colour modes

No single mode carries the full palette. Each medium has a preferred mode, so match the values to where the work will live.

ModeApplicationNotes
HEX / RGBScreenThe truest, most vibrant depiction of the palette. Use for every digital surface.
PantonePrintPreferred for print. Matches the brand colours most reliably across stocks. Official values to be added.
CMYKPrintUse only when Pantone is unavailable, matching to Pantone where possible. Official values to be added.

Accessibility

Clinical Ink on Bone clears WCAG AA for body text. Signal Orange is for large text, icons and accents. Pair small text with ink or cream, never orange on orange.

TextCream on Ink · Pass AAA
TextInk on Bone · Pass AAA

Incorrect usage

The palette is fixed. Keep colours pure, keep contrast high, and let Signal Orange stay the accent.

✗
