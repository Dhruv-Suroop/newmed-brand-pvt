## 3. Presentation

Slide titles with one supporting line each. Enough for a 10-slide deck.

1. **Building workforce-ready healthcare talent** — Sourcing, training, licensing and deployment, run as one process.
2. **The gap is not supply. It is readiness.** — Hospitals are not short of applicants. They are short of applicants who can start.
3. **Where hiring actually stalls** — Verification, licensing and onboarding, not the interview.
4. **One process, six stages** — Source · Assess · Train · License · Deploy · Support.
5. **Assessment before ambition** — We test clinical English and competency before anyone pays for an exam.
6. **Prometric and DataFlow, handled** — Preparation, scheduling, primary source verification, submission.
7. **Ready on day one** — Documentation standards, escalation protocols and handover format, learned before the first shift.
8. **We stay after deployment** — Ninety days of structured check-ins with the nurse and the unit.
9. **What partnership looks like** — A named coordinator, a shared timeline, and no surprises.
